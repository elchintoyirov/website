I'm Elchin Toyirov, a backend engineer from Tashkent. This is the first post here, so it seems fair to start by saying what this place is for.

## Why a blog

Most of what I learn while building things ends up scattered — half-finished notes, chat messages, commit descriptions nobody reads twice. Writing something down properly is the part that makes it stick, and it forces me to notice the gaps in my own understanding. If a post also saves someone else an afternoon of debugging, so much the better.

## What to expect

Notes on the things I build, the tools I keep coming back to, and the problems that took far longer to solve than they should have. No fixed schedule and no fixed length — a post goes up when there is something worth writing down.

If you want to know who is writing this, the [about](@/pages/about.md) page has the rest.
